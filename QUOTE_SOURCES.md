# Daily quote sources

The daily quote data comes from *Many Thoughts of Many Minds: A Treasury of Quotations from the Literature of Every Land and Every Age*, compiled by Louis Klopsch and published by the Christian Herald in 1896. The original book is available as a digitized edition in [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=10zppbb4A1UC).

The data contains 2,091 distinct excerpts of 200 characters or fewer. Each author attribution is reproduced from the 1896 compilation. Line breaks were joined for display, and repeated text was removed. The compilation includes historical spellings and source attributions that may not meet modern standards; the app presents them as attributed by Klopsch's book.
